OVERVIEW
See how long every card has been in its current list, with color thresholds tailored to each phase.

DESCRIPTION
## See where work is slowing down

Productivity adds a live time badge to every Trello card. At a glance, your team can see how long a card has stayed in its current list and which work needs attention.

![Elapsed-time badges on a sample board](https://productivity-trello-powerup.pages.dev/assets/listing-board.png)

## Set the pace for every phase

Open a list’s **…** menu and choose **Productivity · Time thresholds …**. Set independent green, orange and red thresholds in hours or days. Each list keeps its own settings, so an Ideas list can use a different pace from Review or In progress.

![Per-list green, orange and red thresholds](https://productivity-trello-powerup.pages.dev/assets/listing-settings.png)

### What you get

- Live card-front and card-detail badges, refreshed about every minute.
- Accurate calendar time from the card’s most recent recorded entry into its current list.
- Independent green, orange and red thresholds for every Trello list.
- Shared board settings so the whole team sees the same status.
- Clear guidance to move a card once if Trello history cannot prove its start time.

Productivity is free and has no ads, analytics, subscription or separate account. It requests read-only Trello access to load card history. Processing happens in your browser; cards are never changed or sent to an application backend.

REVIEW INSTRUCTIONS
1. Add the Power-Up to a test board containing two or more lists and cards.
2. Choose Connect to Trello and approve read-only access for your own Trello account.
3. Inspect card-front and card-detail badges. They may briefly show “Loading phase time…”; within ten seconds, recorded creation, board-copy or move history should show the current stay.
4. Move a card to another list; its current stay restarts from that recorded move.
5. Open ... on a list > Productivity · Time thresholds ... and choose Hours.
6. Set green to 0, orange to 0.01 and red to 0.02. Save. A newly created card is green, orange after one minute, and red after two minutes.
7. Empty all three fields for an uncolored elapsed-time badge. Enabled thresholds must increase: green < orange < red.
8. Reload the board: list settings persist. Different lists have independent settings.
9. Use Manage Trello connection > Remove connection to disconnect. Revoke authorization in https://trello.com/my/account as well.

LIMITS
Only badges are colored. Calendar time includes nights and weekends and is not paused by completion or archival. Large boards and rate limits can delay loading. Each member authorizes separately. If Trello has no recorded entry, move the card to another list and back once to start its timer. No developer credentials or extra test account are needed for reviewers.
